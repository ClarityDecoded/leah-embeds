Deep Story

Let's finish what you started.

Deep Story is an intimate storytelling sanctuary for up to six people — a place to complete your stories: to publish, to perform, or simply for the pleasure of sharing.

Deep Story grew out of a request from storytellers who weren't ready to leave their stories behind. At the end of a Wisdom Stories course, a few felt they had only just hit the gold of their story, and wanted to keep digging until they found the end of it… or, in their particular cases, their books. And so that group went renegade and made a new space for story development, called Deep Story.

A place where people can dig in and focus on bringing one story to completion. Through generous listening, thoughtful inquiry, and the practice of writing and rewriting, we have journeyed through our stories—and often, into the next chapter of our lives.

Together we meet sacred storytelling as a practice, tending both the process it takes to bring your story from concept to completion, and the creator — you — and what you go through in bringing a story to life.

You may share up to ten pages, double spaced, each week — as we practice uncovering the wisdom, medicine, deeper truth, and hilarity hidden within our lived experiences, and discover how to shape these stories so that they can serve others.

These sessions may touch the deepest parts of our stories. They can be therapeutic, but they are not therapy.

For Alumni

Deep Story is an opportunity to complete what you started in a course at The School for Sacred Storytelling or somewhere else. Bring a story you started and are inspired to publish or produce. This is a place where you can refine it for publication, for performance, or simply for the satisfaction of answering the call of a story that needs completing.

If You Are New

This program is built on the practice of Sacred Storytelling. You are welcome here. It is not required, but strongly suggested, that you attend a course at The School for Sacred Storytelling or listen to the Myth, Magic & Medicine audio program so you are familiar with some of the exercises and approaches that may be recommended.

You will also be asked to review our program agreements before we begin.

About Leah Lamb

Leah Lamb is an award-winning storyteller, teacher, and founder of the School for Sacred Storytelling. For more than two decades, she has guided thousands of people—from artists to activists, educators, changemakers, moms and dads—in discovering the deeper story that is asking to be lived through them.

She bridges mythology, psychology, contemplative practice, and the timeless craft of storytelling, and specializes in helping people uncover the deeper mythos beneath the events of their lives—the hidden patterns, initiations, and meaning that reveal who they are becoming.

Leah has taught storytelling at Bioneers, Hollyhock, Google, and others. Her storytelling has taken the form of award-winning environmental PSAs, documentaries, feature articles, and plays. Her work has appeared in National Geographic News Watch, Fast Company, Spirituality & Health Magazine, and The Huffington Post.
