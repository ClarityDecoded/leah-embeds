What Is a Soul Story?

A Soul Story is a form of mystic storytelling and divination.

Like tarot, dreamwork, or other intuitive practices, it creates a bridge between ordinary consciousness and the deeper dimensions of who you are.

But rather than receiving a series of separate images or messages, you receive an entire story—woven through spoken word, chant, and song.

The characters, landscapes, colors, objects, animals, challenges, and gifts within the story often give form to experiences already living within your conscious, unconscious, or energetic field.

The story may reflect how you are moving through relationship, how you meet transition, blocks and gifts around purpose and power. It can help make visible what you already sense but have not yet been able to clearly see.

A Soul Story does not tell you what you should do or who you should become. It helps you encounter more of who you already are—and what parts of yourself may be ready to emerge.

Who Receives a Soul Story?

People receive Soul Stories at many different moments in their lives.

Some choose one as a gift to themselves at a birthday, beginning, turning point, or important threshold.

Couples receive a Soul-to-Soul Love Story, creating a shared mythic language they can return to when their relationship is flowing—and when it is not.

A Soul Story may be received by an individual, a couple, a family, or a circle.
