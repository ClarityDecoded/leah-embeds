The Threshold

You are ready to bring forward the parts of you that may have remained separate from your work: your intuition, creative expression, sensitivity, spiritual intelligence, power, and relationship with the unseen.

Every threshold asks something of us.

  • There may be an identity that you have outgrown.
  • A way of being that has become too small.
  • A gift that hasn’t been fully developed.
  • A refusal to keep saying, “I’ll do it tomorrow…”
  • A vision that is ready to move from the unseen into form.

Within the circle, you will name the real threshold you are facing.

Together, we will explore what is ending, what is emerging, what is asking to be reclaimed, and what will support you in taking the next step.

Your Soul Story offers a deeper narrative for your crossing. You will have the time, space, support, structure, and accountability to explore each chapter and apply the practices in your everyday life.

The power of the circle

The Luminary Circle brings together a small group of people who are each carrying something meaningful into the world.

You may be creating a new offering, writing a book, evolving a business, stepping into greater leadership, or changing the way you serve.

The details may differ, but the deeper work is shared:

How do I become the person who can carry what is asking to come through me?

How do I create from connection and alignment rather than force?

How do I bring my intuition, imagination, and spiritual intelligence into the practical structures of my life?

How do I meet this moment without abandoning the deepest parts of myself?

Our gatherings are a place to give and receive support within a group of people who are on the journey with you and committed to your well-being.

Your Guide

Leah Lamb, MSW, is a Story Practitioner. She has given hundreds of Soul Stories to individuals, groups, and couples over the last 16 years.

Her prior work as a social worker involved crisis intervention and guiding youth at risk through the wilderness for seven years.

Her love and desire to care for our planet has expressed itself as producing The Green Channel for Al Gore’s Current TV, covering the UN Climate Talks in Paris and Copenhagen, and creating The Whale Council and the Whale Dreamer book series. Her writing has been featured in National Geographic News Watch, Fast Company, and Spirituality & Health Magazine.

Leah is the founder of The School for Sacred Storytelling, and has led Sacred Storytelling Ceremonies around the country and presented at Bioneers, Hollyhock, and Google.

Her studies led her to The Neighborhood Playhouse, Williamstown Theater Festival, The Orphan Wisdom School, and The 13 Moon Mystery School—as well as many other guides and teachers, among them the forests and Ocean.
